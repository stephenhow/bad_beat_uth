Pay tables

CotesGainBad beat

Gain pays the Blind when you beat the dealer. Bad Beat pays the Blind instead of losing it when the player's hand loses. Blind pushes on other wins. Ante pushes if the dealer doesn't qualify. On a $500 Blind, 500 to 1 pays $250,000.

How a hand works

  1. $500 on the Ante and $500 on the Blind. Raise 4× ($2,000) before the flop, or check.
  2. If you checked, bet 2× ($1,000) on the flop, or check again.
  3. If you still haven't bet, bet 1× ($500) or fold on the river.
  4. You can bet only once. Folding loses the Ante and Blind.
